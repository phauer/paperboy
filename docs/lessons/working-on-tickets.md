# Lessons: Working on Tickets

## Tick Only What Is Really Done

Check each acceptance criterion against what was built before ticking it. If a criterion was dropped or changed, say so in the ticket instead of ticking it. Ticket 18 once had "status shows the last send" ticked although it wasn't built.

## Fold Tickets When the Findings Make Them Small

A spike can turn a follow-up ticket into one criterion of another. The Kindle titles (ticket 19) needed no EPUB change, only the title and author passed at send time. So they moved into the push ticket (18), and 19 was closed with a pointer.

## Park Instead of Finishing for Completeness

Drop comparison work once a working option is accepted, and note it as a fallback. The email route for the Kindle was parked that way in ticket 17. Ticket 08 (podcast threshold) is parked until thin posts actually show up.
